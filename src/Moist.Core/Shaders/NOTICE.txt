Bundled GPU upscaling shaders
=============================

These GLSL shaders run through FFmpeg's libplacebo filter (custom_shader_path) to give
moist a GPU, neural-network upscale ("superres" filter). They are third-party works,
redistributed here under their original MIT licenses.

- FSRCNNX_x2_8-0-4-1.glsl
  FSRCNNX super-resolution shader by igv.
  https://github.com/igv/FSRCNN-TensorFlow   (MIT License)
  General / live-action luma upscaler.

- Anime4K_Upscale_CNN_x2_M.glsl
  Anime4K by bloc97 and contributors.
  https://github.com/bloc97/Anime4K          (MIT License)
  Anime / line-art upscaler.

See each project's repository for the full license text and attribution.
